# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Akrista (the author) writing and organizing personal/public knowledge, and curious readers exploring interconnected ideas on the web.

## Product Purpose

A personal knowledge base (PKB) and digital garden to publish thoughts and organize ideas. Built on Obsidian and Quartz, designed to showcase learning in public in a robust, resilient, and interconnected manner.

## Brand Personality

Organic and Solid: Minimalist, natural stone/soil tones, clean and durable typography. Grounded, resilient, and focused on clarity and readability without unnecessary noise.

## Anti-references

SaaS/AI clichés (ghost cards, tiny tracked uppercase eyebrows, cream/beige backgrounds), generic default Quartz layout/styling, and overly complex, distracting animations.

## Design Principles

- **Solid Foundations**: UI elements should feel structurally sound, grounded, and lasting, drawing inspiration from natural stone and organic textures.
- **Interconnected Clarity**: Visual layout and links must reinforce the web of connections between ideas, making navigation feel intuitive and smooth.
- **Readability First**: Prioritize typography, line length, and contrast so the reading experience is comfortable and distraction-free.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Strict contrast ratios (≥4.5:1 for body, ≥3:1 for large text), responsive layouts for all screen sizes, and consideration for reduced motion (instant transitions / crossfades).
