# Earthquake Recovery & Rebuild / Recuperación y Reconstrucción del Terremoto

<details>
<summary><b>Read in English</b> (Click to expand)</summary>

On June 24, 2026, a significant earthquake struck Venezuela, causing structural damage to my home. This page serves as a public log to document the recovery, repairs, and rebuilding efforts.

## 📋 Context & Damage Assessment

### Apartment Reconstruction Scope

- **Wall Demolition**: Severe damage requires knocking down (_tumbar_) 7 partition walls inside our apartment.
- **Plumbing Repairs**: A broken water pipe inside one of the damaged walls has been capped (_clausurado_). Work is currently underway to route and install the new replacement pipeline.
- **Renovations**: General remodeling, plastering, and masonry repairs are being carried out across the apartment.

### Building-Wide Engineering Scope

- **Elevator Inspections**: Coordinate with certified technicians to inspect and verify elevator safety.
- **Garage Water Filtration**: Inspect and resolve a chronic water filtration issue in the garage that has persisted for 15 years.
- **Beam Fractures**: Repair and reinforce structural beams (_vigas_) on the west wing of the building that sustained minor hairline cracks (_fisuras_) of roughly 1 mm.
